About

A little about me and what I work with.

I'm finishing my Master of Science in Data Science. I like projects where the hardest part is figuring out what the data is really saying, whether that's which customers are likely to buy, which schools perform best, or how the economy affects loan risk.

Most of my work follows the same steps: clean the data carefully, try a simple model first, compare it to something stronger, and explain the results in plain language so people who aren't technical can use them.

I'm looking for a role in data science or data analytics where I can build models and dashboards that help people make better decisions.
